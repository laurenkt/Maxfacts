description:
    - Serve with leek sauce and mashed butter beans (passed through a sieve, add a little (olive) oil)
ingredients:
    - 200g tinned tuna in water, drained
    - 4 eggs, separated
    - salt and white pepper
    - 'butter for greasing '
method:
    - Preheat the oven to 180°C and grease a cake tin, set aside. Add the tuna flakes to the yolks and puree the mixture, pass through a sieve, season to taste. Stiffly whip the egg whites and fold into the tuna puree. Pour mixture into the greased tin, place tin in a roasting tin and add hot water to the roasting tin about half way up the side of the cake tin. Bake for about 45 minutes.
