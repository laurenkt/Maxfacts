title: Watermelon juice
tags:
  - sweet

description: []
ingredients:
    - 500 g ripe watermelon pieces
    - small handful of mint leaves
    - 1 tbsp sugar (optional, to taste)
    - 100 ml water (or coconut water)
method:
    - Blend all ingredients to a smooth consistency, pass through a fine-meshed sieve. Serve chilled (or with ice).
variations:
    - For a naughty version of this refreshing drink, add a shot of white rum or brandy. The liquid mixture (with or without alcohol) can also be made into a jelly with ordinary gelatin (takes several hours of refrigeration to set). Omit the sugar but add a scoop of vanilla ice cream before blending.
