description:
    - A clafoutis is the French version of an oven-baked pancake; it is traditionally prepared with cherries but also works for other ripe summer fruit, such as pear, apricots or peaches.
ingredients:
    - generous knob of soft butter
    - sugar, to sprinkle buttered dish
    - 2-3 ripe pears, peeled, cored and quartered
    - 2 tbsp flour
    - 1 tbsp vanilla sugar
    - 100 ml milk
    - 50 ml cream
    - 2 eggs, lightly beaten
method:
    - 'Generously butter a flat gratin dish, sprinkle with sugar, set aside. Preheat oven to 180°C. Place prepared pears in buttered dish. In a bowl, mix beaten eggs with milk, cream, vanilla sugar and flour. When well mixed, pour over pears and bake for 45 minutes. Tastes good hot, lukewarm and cold. '
