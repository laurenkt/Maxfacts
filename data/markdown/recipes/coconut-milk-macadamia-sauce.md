tags:

description:
    - 'This no-cook, cold sauce is very versatile, particularly good with all kinds of vegetable dishes. '
ingredients:
    - 100 g macadamia nuts
    - 100 ml vegetable stock
    - 100 ml coconut milk, unsweetened
    - 1 tbsp rapeseed oil
    - 2 tbsp soy sauce
    - spritz of lime or lemon juice
    - 1 garlic clove, prepared
    - 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
    - salt, pepper to taste
method:
    - Blend all ingredients to a smooth consistency, pass through a fine-meshed sieve. Adjust thickness as preferred, season to taste.
