ingredients:
    - 250 g soft prunes, chopped
    - 500 ml aromatic red wine
    - 1 onion, finely chopped
    - generous knob of butter
    - salt and white pepper to taste
    - '[optional: cream or crème fraiche]'
method:
    - In a saucepan gently heat wine until lukewarm, add chopped prunes and set aside to soak prunes. In another saucepan melt the butter, add chopped onion and sweat until soft. Add wine / prune mixture, blend until smooth, pass through a fine-meshed sieve, season to taste. Adjust thickness according to preference. If preferred, add cream or crème fraiche before serving.
