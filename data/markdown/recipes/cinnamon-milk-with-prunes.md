title: Cinnamon milk with prunes
tags:
  - sweet
  - lukewarm
  - cold
  - liquid
description:
    - Prunes are a great source of fibre.
ingredients:
    - 500ml whole milk
    - 100ml water
    - 100g soft prunes, chopped
    - a good pinch of ground cinnamon
    - 2-3 tbsp caster sugar, to taste
method:
    - Gently heat all ingredients in saucepan, puree and strain (if necessary). Tastes best lukewarm.
