title: Mouth & throat soothers – frozen infusions
tags:
  - frozen
description:
    - These ice granules are a refreshing mouth soother.
ingredients:
    - 5 tbsp of equal amounts of fresh rosemary, thyme, marjoram and sage
    - 1 cinnamon stick
    - 3 cloves
    - 1l boiling water
method:
    - Pour the boiling water over the herb and spice mixture, cover and let infuse for about 10 minutes. Strain and put aside to cool. Pour into a shallow dish and place in the freezer. Stir the mixture with a fork when ice crystals start forming, after about 1 hour. Repeat stirring occasionally until all liquid is frozen and you have a mass of ice crystals / granules. The more often you stir, the finer the granules will be. Store in a lidded plastic container in the freezer.
variations:
    - Use a camomile infusion and add a small amount of honey while still hot.
