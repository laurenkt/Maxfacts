title: Kale – pineapple smoothie 
tags:
  - savoury
  - liquid

description: []
ingredients:
    - 100 g kale leaves, washed and roughly chopped
    - 50 g ripe pineapple chunks
    - 50 g banana
    - 50 g Greek yogurt
method:
    - Mix all ingredients, blend to a smooth consistency, pass through a fine-meshed sieve and adjust thickness according to preference (with a little water or more yogurt). Serve chilled.
