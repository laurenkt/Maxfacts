ingredients:
    - 500g watermelon pieces
    - pinch of ground cinnamon
    - 50g caster sugar
    - 1 tbsp lemon juice
    - powdered gelatin
method:
    - Soak gelatin in a little water. Puree water melon with sugar, cinnamon and lemon juice. Gently warm puree, add dissolved gelatin while stirring. Set aside to cool, refrigerate for several hours.
variations:
    - This refreshing jelly goes well with (sweetened) milk, (whipped) cream, yogurt or custard. It is equally good with (cold) blue cheese sauce. A softly set, wobbly watermelon jelly is a useful lubricant for other foods, similar to wobbly cucumber jelly.
