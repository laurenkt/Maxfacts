description:
    - A smooth and gentle fish taste to accompany vegetables / vegetable purees; particularly nice with green asparagus, but also a good lubricant for boiled potatoes. Quick and easy to prepare, no cooking.
ingredients:
    - '5 tbsp good quality mayonnaise (best homemade) '
    - 2 tbsp Greek yogurt
    - spritz of lemon juice
    - 3 tbsp white wine (or vermouth)
    - 2 anchovy fillets, rinsed and drained, chopped
    - 100 g smoked trout filet, roughly chopped
method:
    - Combine all ingredients, blend to a smooth consistency. Serve immediately.
