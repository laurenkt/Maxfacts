title: Basil - buttermilk soup 
tags:
  - savoury
  - cold
  - smooth
description:
    - Very easy and quick to prepare, this aromatic cold soup can also be enjoyed with a sore mouth or throat.
ingredients:
    - small handful of washed and finely chopped white parts of leek
    - large bunch of fresh basil
    - 500 ml buttermilk
    - salt, white pepper to taste
    - 'spritz of lemon juice '
method:
    - Blend prepared leeks, roughly chopped basil leaves and buttermilk to a smooth green soup, pass through a fine-meshed sieve. Season to taste with a little salt and pepper, and a spritz of lemon juice (if liked). Serve chilled.
