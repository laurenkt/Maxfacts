ingredients:
    - 350g spinach
    - 1 onion, chopped
    - 200g paneer, cubed
    - 1 tsp ground cumin
    - thick slice of fresh ginger, peeled and very finely chopped
    - 2 garlic cloves, finely chopped
    - 2 tsp ground coriander
    - small pinch of ground cardamom
    - ca. 4 tbsp crème fraiche
    - salt, lemon juice to taste
method:
    - Wash the spinach leaves and wilt in a saucepan with a lid, drain well when soft, blend to a soft puree, set aside.
    - Heat a little (rapeseed) oil in a large pan, add onion, ginger, garlic and spices. Cook over low heat until soft. Puree the onion / spice mixture, combine with the spinach puree. Adjust thickness with crème fraiche and possibly a little water for a thick creamy sauce. Gently reheat, add paneer cubes and simmer for 5 minutes. Season to taste with salt and lemon juice.
