title: Melon – cream cheese mousse 
tags:
  - sweet
  - soft
  - smooth
  - mousse
  - cold
description:
    - Particularly nice when served with extra melon puree.
ingredients:
    - 500 g Savoy cabbage leaves, prepared and shredded
    - 150 g potatoes, peeled and chopped
    - 1 onion, peeled and chopped
    - generous knob of butter
    - 1 l vegetable stock (or chicken stock, if preferred)
    - 1 unwaxed orange, finely grated zest
    - handful of flat-leaf parsley, chopped
    - 50 g walnut pieces, finely chopped
    - 100 g crème fraiche
    - salt, white pepper
method:
    - In a saucepan, melt butter over moderate heat. Add onion and sweat for a few minutes. Add potato and Savoy cabbage, sweat for a few more minutes while stirring. Add stock and bring to the boil. Reduce heat slightly and cook for about 20 minutes until cabbage and potato are soft.
    - Remove from heat, add orange zest, parsley and walnut pieces. Blend to a smooth consistency, pass through a fine-meshed sieve. Add crème fraiche, adjust thickness as preferred and season to taste.
