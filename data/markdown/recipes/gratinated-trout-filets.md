ingredients:
    - '300 g trout filets, without skin and deboned '
    - 'generous knob of butter '
    - pinch of ground coriander
    - 2 egg yolks
    - 100 ml single cream
    - sprinkle of vermouth (noilly Prat or dry Martini Bianco)
    - salt, white pepper to taste
method:
    - 'Preheat oven to 220-240°C. Cut trout filets into slices and place in well-buttered flat gratin dish. Season with coriander, salt and pepper to taste, sprinkle with a little vermouth. Mix egg yolks and cream, pour over fish filets. Place in upper section of hot oven and bake for 5-10 minutes (until slightly browned). '
