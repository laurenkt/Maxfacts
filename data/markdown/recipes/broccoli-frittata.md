ingredients:
    - 400 g pre-cooked broccoli in small pieces (or mixture of broccoli and cauliflower)
    - generous knob of soft butter
    - 4 eggs
    - 100 ml milk (or single cream)
    - salt, white pepper to taste
    - handful of finely grated cheddar or Gruyère cheese
method:
    - Generously grease shallow baking dish with soft butter, add vegetable pieces and spread out in one layer. Preheat oven to 180°C. In a bowl whisk eggs, milk and seasoning, pour over vegetables. Sprinkle top with grated cheese. Bake for 40 minutes.
