title: Grape juice cocktail

description: []
ingredients:
    - 100 g Greek yogurt
    - 200 ml grape juice (white)
    - small handful of sage leaves
    - 3 tbsp runny honey
    - 250 ml sparkling mineral water (or still, if preferred)
method:
    - Combine all ingredients, blend to a smooth consistency, pass through a fine-meshed sieve. Adjust thickness as preferred, serve chilled (or add a few ice cubes).
