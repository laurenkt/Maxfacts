description: []
ingredients:
    - 1 apple, peeled, cored, chopped
    - ½ ripe banana, peeled chopped
    - 'spritz of lemon juice '
    - ½ tsp ground cinnamon
    - 2 handful baby leaf spinach, washed
    - water, as needed (or milk, if preferred)
method:
    - Combine all ingredients, blend to a smooth consistency, pass through a fine-meshed sieve, adjust thickness as preferred. Serve chilled.
