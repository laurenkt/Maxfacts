ingredients:
    - a handful of baby spinach leaves or lambs lettuce, washed
    - a small handful of lemon balm leaves
    - 1 small apple, peeled and cored, chopped
    - ½ cucumber, peeled and de-seeded, chopped
    - 1 ripe banana, peeled and chopped
    - coconut milk (or milk)
    - water
method:
    - First puree the green leaves with a little water. Add apple, cucumber pieces, banana and some coconut milk (or milk) and puree again. Strain and adjust thickness with cold water. Serve immediately.
