ingredients:
    - 200g white onions, peeled and chopped
    - a knob of butter
    - 1tsp sugar
    - 50ml white wine
    - 400ml chicken stock
    - 2 bay leaves
    - 1 clove garlic, sliced
    - 2 slices fresh ginger
    - 300ml cream
    - 20g soft butter
    - salt and ground nutmeg
method:
    - Melt butter in saucepan, add sugar, then chopped onion. Sweat for a few minutes. Add wine and reduce liquid. Add chicken stock, ginger and bay leaves, simmer for 30-45 minutes until onions are very soft. Remove bay leaves and ginger, add cream and soft butter, puree the mixture. Pass through a sieve, adjust thickness and season to taste.
