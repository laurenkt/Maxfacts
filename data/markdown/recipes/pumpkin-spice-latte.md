description:
    - Why not include some hyped (2016/2017) must-have drink in our collection… If a sweeter version is preferred, simply omit the espresso (or strong coffee). Nice way to use up left-over pumpkin puree.
ingredients:
    - '2 tbsp maple syrup '
    - pinch of ground cinnamon, ground ginger, ground nutmeg and ground cloves each
    - ca. 5 tbsp pumpkin puree
    - 1 shot espresso (or same quantity of other very strong black coffee)
    - 150 ml whole milk
    - 50 ml whipped cream
method:
    - Mix maple syrup and spices in a mug, add espresso (or other strong coffee). Gently heat milk and 3 tbsp of pumpkin puree in a saucepan (do not boil) while whisking; when hot, add to espresso / spice mixture in mug. Mix whipped cream with rest of pumpkin puree, use to top mixture in mug.
