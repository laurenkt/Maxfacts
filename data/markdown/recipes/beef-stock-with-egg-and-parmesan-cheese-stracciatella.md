  - soft
description: []
ingredients:
    - 500ml beef stock (shop-bought or home-made, avoid stock cubes for this recipe)
    - 1 egg
    - 1 heaped tbsp finely grated Parmesan cheese
    - pinch of salt, pinch of ground nutmeg
method:
    - Heat beef stock to boiling point. Break egg into a small bowl, whisk and add Parmesan, salt and nutmeg. Slowly pour egg mixture into boiling beef stock while whisking. Continue for 2-3 minutes until egg turns fluffy. Serve immediately.
