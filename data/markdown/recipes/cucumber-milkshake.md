description: []
ingredients:
    - 150 g Greek yogurt
    - 200 ml milk
    - 200 g cucumber, prepared (peeled, seeds removed, chopped)
    - finely grated zest of ½ lemon
    - 2 tbsp runny honey (if liked)
method:
    - Mix and blend all ingredients (with or without honey) and pass through fine-meshed sieve. Serve chilled.
