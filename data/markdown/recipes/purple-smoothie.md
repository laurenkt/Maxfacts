ingredients:
    - 150 g black currants
    - 200 fennel, prepared and chopped
    - 2 apples, sweet variety, peeled, cored and chopped
    - 150 g Greek yogurt
    - 500 ml mineral water
method:
    - Combine all ingredients, blend to a smooth consistency, pass through a fine-meshed sieve. Serve chilled.
variations:
    - Use blueberries instead of black currants.
