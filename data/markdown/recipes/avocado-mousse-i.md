description:
    - If a mixture of food textures is not a problem, serve with some good quality smoked salmon; a very good flavour combination. If that is not an option, also tastes good on its own.
ingredients:
    - 4 spring onions, prepared and chopped
    - handful of watercress leaves
    - 2 ripe avocados, peeled cored and chopped
    - 100 g Philadephia (or other) cream cheese
    - 1 tsp prepared mustard
    - 1 tsp runny honey
    - 1 tbsp white aceto balsamico (which is not acidic)
    - 100 ml whipping cream, whipped
    - salt, white pepper
    - ½ sachet powdered gelatin
method:
    - 'In a small saucepan, sprinkle gelatin over a little water, set aside and let soak. '
    - Combine avocado, watercress, spring onions, cream cheese, mustard, honey and white balsamico. Blend to a smooth consistency, season to taste. Dissolve soaked gelatin and slowly add to avocado mixture while whisking. Pass mixture through a fine-meshed sieve. Fold in whipped cream. Refrigerate for several hours until set. Serve chilled.
