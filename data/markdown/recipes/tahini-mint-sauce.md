description:
    - A good side dish / sauce for many occasions, also nice as a snack on its own.
ingredients:
    - 3 tbsp Tahini paste
    - splash of lemon juice
    - '½ ripe avocado, chopped '
    - ½ courgette, seeds removed, chopped
    - 1 garlic clove
    - handful of fresh mint leaves
    - handful of fresh basil leaves
    - '2 Medjool dates (optional) '
    - a little water
method:
    - Blend all ingredients to a smooth consistency, adjust thickness by adding a little water. Serve chilled.
