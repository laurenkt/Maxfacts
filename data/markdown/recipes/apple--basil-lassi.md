description: []
ingredients:
    - 1 apple, peeled, cored, chopped
    - 250 g Greek yogurt
    - 100 ml mineral water, sparkling
    - 1 tbsp runny honey (optional)
    - 1 lime (or lemon), finely grated zest
    - handful of basil leaves, shredded
    - pinch of salt
method:
    - 'Combine apple, yogurt, mineral water, honey (if used) and lime zest, blend until smooth. Add shredded basil leaves and salt, blend again. Pass through a fine-meshed sieve. Serve chilled. '
variations:
    - If spicy food is not a problem, a dash of tabasco sauce (or other hot chili sauce) works well in this lassi.
