ingredients:
    - 200 g beetroot, pre-cooked, chopped
    - generous knob of butter
    - 150 ml whipping cream
    - 'salt and white pepper '
method:
    - Melt butter in saucepan, add beetroot pieces and cook over low heat for 5 minutes, stirring. Add cream, bring to the boil, cook for another 5 minutes. Remove from heat, blend to a smooth consistency, pass through a fine-meshed sieve, season to taste, adjust consistency as preferred.
