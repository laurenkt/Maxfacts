title: Blackberry milk shake
tags:
  - sweet
  - liquid
  - cold
description: []
ingredients:
    - 250 g blackberries, washed and drained
    - 2 tsp fresh rosemary, chopped
    - 750 ml buttermilk
    - squeeze of runny honey
method:
    - Combine all ingredients and blend mixture. Pass through a fine-meshed sieve, serve chilled.
variations:
    - Replace honey by a scoop, or two, of good quality vanilla ice cream and serve immediately.
