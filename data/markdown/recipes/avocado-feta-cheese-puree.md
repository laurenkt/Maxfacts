description: []
ingredients:
    - ½ ripe avocado, prepared
    - 1 garlic clove, very finely chopped
    - 50 feta cheese, crumbled
    - spritz of lemon juice
    - salt, white pepper to taste
method:
    - In a bowl mix all ingredients and smash carefully with a fork. Pass mixture through a fine-meshed sieve to obtain a smooth puree, season to taste, serve immediately.
