title: Peanut sauce 
tags:
  - savoury
  - sauce
  - cold
description:
    - A useful flavour addition to the menu, a versatile sauce for many vegetable purees. Best prepared a day ahead.
ingredients:
    - 100 ml coconut milk (unsweetened)
    - 2 tbsp soy sauce
    - 2 tbsp smooth peanut butter
    - finely grated zest of 1 unwaxed lime
    - 2 slices fresh ginger
method:
    - 'Mix coconut milk, soy sauce, peanut butter and lime zest. Adjust thickness as preferred and add ginger to infuse. Refrigerate for several hours or overnight before serving. '
