title: Banana - strawberry ice cream 
tags:
  - sweet
  - frozen
description: []
ingredients:
    - 120 g Greek yogurt
    - 60 ml full milk
    - 1 tbsp runny honey (optional)
    - 200 g strawberries, prepared and chopped
    - 1 large ripe banana, peeled and chopped
method:
    - Combine all ingredients and blend to a smooth consistency, pass through a fine-meshed sieve. Pour mixture into a freezer-proof container with a lid, freeze for several hours or overnight. Remove from freezer to fridge about 30 minutes before serving.
variations:
    - Omit the freezing step and serve the blended mixture chilled or at room temperature.
