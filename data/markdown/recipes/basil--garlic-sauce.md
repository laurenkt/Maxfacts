ingredients:
    - 'large bunch of basil, leaves only '
    - 250 g quark
    - 2-3 tbsp good-quality mayonnaise
    - finely grated zest of ½ unwaxed lemon
    - 2 garlic cloves, peeled and finely chopped
    - salt, white pepper to taste
    - a few tbsp of milk
method:
    - 'Chop basil leaves, add finely chopped garlic, lemon zest and just enough milk for blending the mixture to a smooth consistency. Pass through a fine-meshed sieve into a bowl, add quark and mayonnaise, mix well, season to taste. '
