title: Virgin colada
tags:
  - sweet
  - liquid
  - cold
description:
    - This is a virtuous = alcohol-free version of a pina colada – on a less virtuous day, you may feel tempted to add a little white rum to the mix… Either way, this is a low-effort, calorie-rich treat.
ingredients:
    - 150 g Greek yogurt
    - 200 ml coconut milk (unsweetened)
    - 200 ml pineapple juice
    - 1 tbsp runny honey
    - 2 tbsp lime juice (optional)
    - ice cubes
method:
    - Combine yogurt, coconut milk, pineapple juice, honey and lime juice (if used) and blend well. Serve chilled with ice cubes added.
