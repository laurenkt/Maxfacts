description:
    - This sauce is full of flavour and is a great flavour-enhancer for many other dishes. If you feel it would work better for you if it was a little more gentle, just add some crème fraiche or thick double cream before serving.
ingredients:
    - 5 tbsp Tahini
    - splash of lemon juice
    - 1 bunch of flat-leaf parsley, leaves only
    - 1 bunch coriander, leaves only
    - pinch of salt
    - a little water
method:
    - Blend all ingredients to a smooth consistency, adjust thickness by adding a little water. Serve chilled.
