ingredients:
    - 1 ripe large avocado, peeled, cored and chopped
    - 1 tbs lime (or lemon) juice
    - 100 ml coconut milk, unsweetened
    - 100 ml cream, whipped
    - pinch of salt
    - ½ sachet powdered gelatin
method:
    - In a small saucepan, sprinkle gelatin over a little water, set aside and let soak. Combine avocado, lime juice and coconut milk, blend until smooth. Pass through a fine-meshed sieve, season to taste. Dissolve soaked gelatin and slowly add to avocado puree while whisking. Fold in whipped cream. Refrigerate for several hours until set. Serve chilled.
