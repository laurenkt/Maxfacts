ingredients:
    - Pimm’s, Campari, Port, Sherry, G & T, Guinness, wine, etc.
    - powdered gelatin
method:
    - Soak and dissolve gelatin in a little water. Prepare your favourite tipple, add dissolved gelatin while stirring. Set aside to cool, then refrigerate for several hours. When using beer, heat the beer gently to de-foam it before adding the gelatin. This preparation with gelatin does not work for concentrated spirits.
