title: Smoked salmon mousse

  - smooth
  - mousse

description:
    - The chives add a nice fresh taste; if ‘bits’ in your food are a problem, just omit the chives or replace by some finely chopped shallot, added before the mixture is blended.
ingredients:
    - 250 g smoked salmon, finely chopped
    - 4 tbsp Philadelphia (or other) cream cheese
    - 1 tbsp cream
    - 1 tbsp lemon juice
    - 3 tbsp finely chopped chives (or ½ finely chopped shallot)
    - salt, white pepper
method:
    - Combine smoked salmon, lemon juice and cream cheese (plus chopped shallot, if used), blend to a smooth consistency. Season to taste, add chives and mix well (if used). Chill for 30 minutes, serve immediately.
