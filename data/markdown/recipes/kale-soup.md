ingredients:
    - 250 g kale leaves, washed and roughly chopped
    - 3 shallots, chopped
    - 1 garlic clove, finely chopped
    - 1 tbsp plain flour
    - generous knob of butter
    - 50 ml white wine
    - 400 ml chicken (or vegetable) stock
    - 200 ml cream
    - salt, white pepper
    - 50 ground almonds
    - 2 tbsp finely grated Parmesan cheese
    - 4 tbsp rapeseed (or virgin olive) oil
method:
    - In a saucepan, melt butter, add flour, whisk continuously and add wine. Continue whisking, add stock and cream. Bring to a gentle boil while stirring, and continue to boil uncovered until liquid is reduced by roughly a third. Remove from the heat, season to taste.
    - In another saucepan bring salted water to the boil and add chopped kale, cook vigorously for 2 minutes, drain. Add drained kale, garlic, almonds, Parmesan and oil to the stock mixture and blend to a smooth consistency. Pass through a fine-meshed sieve. Adjust thickness as preferred.
