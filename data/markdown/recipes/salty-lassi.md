title: Salty lassi

  - liquid
description: []
ingredients:
    - 200ml water
    - 100ml Greek yogurt (or curd, if you can find some)
    - a pinch of salt
    - 1 tsp cumin seeds
method:
    - Fry the cumin seeds in a small dry pan until fragrant. Whisk yogurt and water, add a pinch of salt and the cumin seeds, mix well. Strain and serve chilled.
variations:
    - For a creamier version, replace water by milk.
