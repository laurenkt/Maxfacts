ingredients:
    - 700 g sweet potato, peeled and diced
    - 2 stalks celery, prepared and chopped
    - 1 large onion, finely chopped
    - 1 garlic clove, finely chopped
    - 3 tbsp smooth peanut butter
    - 200 ml coconut milk
    - 900 ml chicken stock
    - handful of fresh coriander leaves, chopped
    - salt, white pepper
    - 3 tbsp soy sauce
    - spritz of lime juice
method:
    - In a saucepan, heat some oil and gently cook onion and garlic until starting to soften. Add celery and sweet potato, sweat for a few more minutes. Add peanut butter, coconut milk and chicken stock. Bring to the boil, reduce heat and simmer for 30 minutes. Remove from heat, add coriander leaves. Blend to a smooth consistency, pass through a fine-meshed sieve. Adjust thickness as preferred, add soy sauce and a little lime juice, season to taste.
variations:
    - If the mixture is kept fairly thick, it also works well as a sauce or puree.
