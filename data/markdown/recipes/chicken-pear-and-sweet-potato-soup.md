ingredients:
    - 1 chicken breast, skinned and deboned, diced
    - 1 pear, cores and peeled, diced
    - 1 sweet potato, peeled and diced
    - 1 small courgette, diced
    - 500ml chicken stock
    - salt, white pepper to taste
method:
    - 'Bring stock to boil, add chicken pieces, reduce heat and simmer for 10 minutes. Add sweet potato and pear pieces, continue to simmer for another 10 minutes. Add courgette pieces, continue to simmer for another 5-10 minutes. Remove from stove, puree mixture, pass through sieve, adjust thickness and season to taste. '
