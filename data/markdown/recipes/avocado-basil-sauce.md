ingredients:
    - handful of basil leaves
    - 1 garlic clove, chopped
    - 1 ripe avocado, peeled, cored and chopped
    - 1 tbsp lemon or lime juice
    - 150 ml double cream
    - 75 ml milk
    - 1 tsp caster sugar
    - salt, white pepper to taste
method:
    - Mix prepared avocado pieces with lemon (or lime) juice to prevent them turning brown. Add basil leaves, garlic, sugar, cream and milk and blend mixture to a smooth consistency. Pass through a fine-meshed sieve, season to taste. Adjust thickness as preferred. Serve chilled.
