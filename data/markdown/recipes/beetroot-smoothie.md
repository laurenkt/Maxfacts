ingredients:
    - 150g Greek yogurt
    - 150ml whole milk (or buttermilk or cream)
    - grated zest of ½ lemon
    - salt and white pepper to taste
    - 3 tbsp cooked beetroot, chopped
    - 3 tbsp chopped pear
    - handful of lambs lettuce (or handful of other chopped tender salad leaves)
method:
    - Combine all ingredients and puree together, strain. Serve chilled.
