ingredients:
    - '200ml cream '
    - 100-150g Gorgonzola cheese (not the creamy variety which is called Gorgonzola dolce)
method:
    - Heat the cream in a saucepan nearly to boiling point, crumble the Gorgonzola cheese, add to the cream and keep stirring until smooth, do not overheat. Serve immediately.
variations:
    - If you prefer a stronger blue cheese taste, use Roquefort or Stilton cheese.
