ingredients:
    - 500ml milk
    - 'optional: 2 large strips of lemon rind (unwaxed lemon)'
    - powdered gelatin
method:
    - Gently warm the milk with the lemon rind for 10-15 minutes. Dissolve the gelatin in a little water and slowly pour into the warm milk while stirring. Discard the lemon rind. Stir occasionally until cooled, then pour in small moulds or a larger dish and put in the fridge to set.
variations:
    - 'Infuse the milk with a cinnamon stick or cardamom pods. Make a jellied milkshake: before pouring the cooled milk into moulds, mix it with some smooth fruit puree (same relative amounts as for a milkshake). For a more refreshing taste, use buttermilk instead of milk.'
