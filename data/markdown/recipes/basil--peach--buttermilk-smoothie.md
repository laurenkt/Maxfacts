description: []
ingredients:
    - 300 ml buttermilk, chilled
    - 2 ripe peaches (or nectarines), peeled and chopped
    - 1 bunch fresh basil, leaves only
method:
    - Combine all ingredients and blend mixture, pass through fine-meshed sieve. Serve chilled.
