title: Pear and almond milkshake

  - sweet

  - cold
description: []
ingredients:
    - 1 large ripe pear, peeled cored and chopped
    - 1 soft dried date, chopped
    - 300 ml milk
    - 1 tsp ground cardamom
    - 2 tbsp ground almonds
method:
    - Combine all ingredients, blend to a smooth consistency, pass through a fine-meshed sieve. Serve chilled.
