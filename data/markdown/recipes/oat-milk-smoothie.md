ingredients:
    - 125 g raspberries
    - 1 ripe peach, peeled, stoned and chopped
    - 2 tbsp almond paste (or marzipan)
    - 150 ml oat milk, unsweetened
    - 'pinch of ground cinnamon '
method:
    - Combine all ingredients, blend to a smooth liquid. Pass through a fine-meshed sieve. Serve chilled.
