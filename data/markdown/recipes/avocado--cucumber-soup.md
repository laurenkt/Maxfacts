ingredients:
    - 400 g cucumber, seeds removed, chopped
    - 1 ripe avocado, peeled, cored, chopped
    - spritz of lime (or lemon) juice
    - 3-4 spring onions, prepared and chopped
    - 150 g Greek yogurt
    - small handful of fresh mint (or dill, or coriander) leaves
    - salt
method:
    - Blend all ingredients to a smooth consistency, pass through a fine-meshed sieve. Adjust thickness as preferred, season to taste. Serve chilled
