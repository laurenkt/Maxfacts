description: []
ingredients:
    - 1/2 banana, peeled and chopped
    - 1 large ripe avocado, peeled, cored and chopped
    - 2 handful lettuce leaves, washed and chopped
    - 150 g seedless white grapes
    - 200 ml Greek yogurt
method:
    - Blend all ingredients to a smooth consistency, pass puree through a fine-meshed sieve and serve immediately (puree does not last well).
variations:
    - If a thinner, liquid version is preferred, replace yogurt by buttermilk or dilute yogurt mixture with milk.
