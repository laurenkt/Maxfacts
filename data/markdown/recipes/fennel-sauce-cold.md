description: []
ingredients:
    - 200g fennel, prepared and chopped
    - 1 tbsp fennel seeds
    - 60-80ml crème fraiche
    - knob of butter
    - salt, white pepper
method:
    - Heat butter with a little water in a saucepan, add fennel seeds and fennel, braise until soft. Blend to a puree and pass through a sieve, set aside to cool. Add crème fraiche, mix well, season to taste.
