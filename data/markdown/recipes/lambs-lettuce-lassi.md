description: []
ingredients:
    - '80 g lamb’s lettuce, washed, prepared and drained '
    - 150 g Greek yogurt
    - 100 ml milk
    - 2 tbsp runny honey
    - pinch of salt
    - 150 ml sparkling mineral water, chilled
method:
    - Combine lamb’s lettuce, yogurt, milk, honey and salt. Blend to a smooth consistency, pass through a fine-meshed sieve. Adjust thickness with sparkling mineral water, mix well. Serve right away.
variations:
    - Replace lamb’s lettuce by rocket.
