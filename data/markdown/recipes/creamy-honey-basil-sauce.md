description:
    - If you feel like giving pasta a try, perhaps with some small pasta shapes, this sauce is a very good candidate. It is also a tasty lubricant for many other dishes, at all temperatures.
ingredients:
    - 2 large handfuls of basil leaves, roughly chopped
    - 3 garlic cloves, chopped
    - 3 tbsp pine nuts; slightly toasted (in a dry pan)
    - 3 tbsp finely grated Parmesan cheese
    - spritz of lemon juice
    - 100 ml rapeseed (or extra-virgin olive) oil
    - 3 tbps runny honey
    - 300 ml whipping (or double) cream
    - 150 ml chicken stock
method:
    - Blend basil leaves, garlic, pine nuts, Parmesan, oil, honey and lemon juice to a smooth puree. In a saucepan heat cream and chicken stock, add basil puree and bring to the boil. Reduce heat slightly and simmer for about 30 minutes until sauce starts to thicken.
