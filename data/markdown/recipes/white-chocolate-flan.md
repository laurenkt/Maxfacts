ingredients:
    - 350ml whole milk
    - 150ml double cream
    - 2 tbsp caster sugar
    - 1 vanilla pod, split and scraped
    - 200g white chocolate, broken into small pieces
    - 4 egg yolks
    - 2 eggs, slightly beaten
method:
    - Preheat oven to 160°C. In a saucepan bring milk, cream, sugar and vanilla to the boil, remove from heat. Add white chocolate while stirring until molten. Add yolks and eggs while stirring. Strain through a fine sieve and divide into small ramekins. Cover each with aluminium foil. Place ramekins in a roasting tin and add hot water to the roasting tin, about 2/3 of height of ramekins. Bake for 25-30 minutes. Serve lukewarm or cold (store in refrigerator).
