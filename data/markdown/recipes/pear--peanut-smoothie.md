description: []
ingredients:
    - 2 ripe pears, peeled, cored, chopped
    - 2 tbsp Greek yogurt
    - 2 tbsp smooth peanut butter
method:
    - Blend all ingredients with 100 ml water until smooth, pass through a fine-meshed sieve, adjust thickness by adding more water (if wanted). Serve chilled.
