description: []
ingredients:
    - 500 g ricotta
    - 2 eggs, lightly beaten
    - finely grated zest of 1 unwaxed lemon
    - 80 g icing sugar
    - knob of butter
method:
    - Preheat oven to 200°C. Grease four ramekins with soft butter, set aside. In a bowl mix ricotta, eggs, lemon zest and icing sugar. Divide mixture into ramekins, bake for 20 minutes until fairly firm and golden brown. Serve lukewarm or cold.
