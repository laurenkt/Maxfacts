ingredients:
    - 400g apple sauce
    - 'grated zest of 1 unwaxed lemon '
    - 4 tbsp honey
    - pinch of ground cinnamon
    - 250g Greek yogurt
    - 100ml whipping cream
method:
    - 'Mix apple sauce with honey, lemon zest, cinnamon and yogurt. Whip the cream and fold into the apple sauce mixture. Freeze in a shallow container and whisk mixture during freezing every half hour until firm. Move from freezer to fridge for about 30 minutes to one hour before serving. '
