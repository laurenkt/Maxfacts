ingredients:
    - 500g spinach
    - 1 small shallot, finely chopped
    - 1 clove garlic, finely chopped
    - a knob of butter
    - 50ml cream
    - salt and white pepper, ground nutmeg
    - powdered gelatin
method:
    - Melt the butter in a saucepan over medium heat, add chopped shallots and garlic and sweat for a few minutes until soft. Add washed (moist) spinach and let wilt. Add cream and simmer until very soft. Puree mixture and season to taste, pass through a sieve if in doubt. Dissolve gelatin and slowly pour into spinach puree. Pour into dish and set aside to cool, then put in fridge to set.
