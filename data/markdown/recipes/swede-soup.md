description:
    - When prepared with less chicken stock and omitting the cream, this can also be used as a swede puree or a thick swede sauce.
ingredients:
    - 750 g swedes, prepared and chopped
    - 250 g carrots, prepared and chopped
    - 1200 ml chicken stock
    - 100 g smoked bacon, thick slice(s)
    - 100 ml cream
    - pinch of ground nutmeg
    - salt, white pepper
    - pumpkin seed oil, to serve
method:
    - In a saucepan, bring stock, swedes, carrots and bacon to the boil. Reduce heat and simmer for 30 minutes until vegetables are soft. Remove from heat, remove bacon slices. Blend mixture to a smooth consistency, add cream. Adjust thickness as preferred and season to taste. Serve with a drizzle of pumpkin seed oil for extra flavour.
