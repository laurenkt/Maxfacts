description: []
ingredients:
    - 200 g good quality vanilla ice cream
    - 100 ml milk
    - 50 g tahini
    - ½ tsp vanilla paste
    - pinch of ground cinnamon
    - 150 ml whipping cream, whipped (optional, to serve)
method:
    - Combine ice cream, milk, tahini and cinnamon and blend to a smooth consistency. Serve immediately, topped with a dollop of whipped cream if liked.
