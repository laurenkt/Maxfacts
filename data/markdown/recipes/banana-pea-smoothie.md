description:
    - Neither savoury nor sweet, really – but a tasty and healthy snack; kids like it too.
ingredients:
    - '1 small banana, peeled and chopped '
    - 150 g peas (fresh or frozen)
    - 100 g soft tofu, chopped
    - handful of mint leaves, roughly chopped
    - 300 ml soy or oat milk (unsweetened) – or milk, if preferred
    - 2 tbsp salted peanuts, chopped
    - ½ tsp powdered aniseeds (optional)
method:
    - Combine all ingredients and blend to a smooth and creamy consistency. Pass through a fine-meshed sieve. Adjust thickness as preferred, serve chilled.
