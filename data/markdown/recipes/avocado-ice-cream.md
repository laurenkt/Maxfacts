ingredients:
    - '3 ripe avocados '
    - juice of 1 lemon
    - 350ml cream of coconut
method:
    - Peel, stone and chop the avocados. Toss in lemon juice and finely puree. Mix with the coconut cream. Freeze for ca. 3 hours. Move from freezer to fridge for about 30 minutes to one hour before serving.
