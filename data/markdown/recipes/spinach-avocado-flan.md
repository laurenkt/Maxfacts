ingredients:
    - 250 g spinach
    - 1 shallot, finely chopped
    - knob of butter
    - 1 garlic clove, finely chopped
    - 1 ripe avocado, prepared
    - 1 egg, lightly beaten
    - 100 ml cream
    - pinch of ground nutmeg
    - salt, white pepper
method:
    - Generously butter a flat oven-proof dish, set aside. Preheat oven to 200°C. Melt butter in a large saucepan, add shallot and sweat until softened. Add washed spinach and wilt it. Drain well when cooked, then chop very finely. Blend prepared avocado, garlic and spinach to a smooth puree, add nutmeg. Season to taste and transfer to prepared baking dish. Mix beaten egg and cream, pour over avocado / spinach puree, bake for 30 minutes. Let sit for a few minutes before serving.
