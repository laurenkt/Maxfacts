description:
    - 'This recipe can double up as a sauce if prepared in a thicker consistency. '
ingredients:
    - 1 shallot, fine chopped
    - 1 stale bread roll (1 day old), chopped
    - 50 g butter
    - 400 ml beef stock
    - '100 ml cream '
    - salt, white pepper
method:
    - In a saucepan, melt butter over medium heat, add shallot and bread cubes, roast until golden. Add stock and cream, reduce heat and simmer for 20 minutes. Remove from heat and blend to a smooth consistency, pass through a fine-meshed sieve. Adjust thickness as preferred, season to taste.
variations:
    - If spicy food is not a problem, add 2 tbsp of grated horseradish when adding stock and cream.
