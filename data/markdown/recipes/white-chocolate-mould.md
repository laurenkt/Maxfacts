ingredients:
    - 600ml whole milk
    - 1 egg
    - 2 tbsp corn flour
    - 100g white chocolate, chopped
method:
    - In a saucepan whisk milk, egg and corn flour. Heat while stirring to just under boiling point. Remove pan from heat. Add chopped white chocolate and stir until chocolate melts. Strain into a bowl and cover surface with cling film (to prevent formation of skin), set aside to cool. Refrigerate until serving.
