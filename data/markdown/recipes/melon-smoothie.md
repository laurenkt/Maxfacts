title: Melon smoothie

description: []
ingredients:
    - 100 g Galia or Cantaloupe melon pieces, chilled
    - 200 ml Greek yogurt (or kefir)
    - small pinch of ground cinnamon
    - small amount of vanilla essence
    - pinch of salt
method:
    - Combine all ingredients, blend to a smooth consistency, pass through fine-meshed sieve. Adjust thickness as preferred, serve chilled (or add some ice cubes before serving).
