description:
    - Good with all kinds of vegetable and fish dishes, adds a nice savoury kick.
ingredients:
    - '1 tbsp plain flour '
    - 2 tbsp butter
    - 1 garlic clove, lightly crushed
    - 50 ml white wine
    - 50-70 g finely grated Parmesan
    - 100 ml cream
method:
    - In a saucepan melt butter, add flour, mix well. Add garlic clove and cook for a few minutes, while stirring constantly. Remove garlic clove. Add wine and stir/whisk for a few more minutes to ensure there are no lumps. Remove from heat, add cream, stir in the grated Parmesan cheese. Serve hot or lukewarm.
