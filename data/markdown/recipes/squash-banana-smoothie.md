title: Squash-banana smoothie
tags:

  - cold

description: []
ingredients:
    - 350g squash, cooked and cubed
    - 1 small banana, peeled and chopped
    - 150ml buttermilk
    - ca. 250ml milk
    - a pinch of ground cinnamon, cloves and nutmeg
    - 1-2 tbsp runny honey
method:
    - Blend squash, banana, buttermilk and spices to a smooth puree, pass through a sieve. Add milk to dilute to wanted thickness. Add honey to taste, mix well. Serve chilled.
